Patch World

Soft bodies on a world that conserves its mass under one moving sun. Each inherits a shape, muscles and a small brain, learns during its own life, and can plan its next moves through its own model of its body. Every birth mutates body and brain. Model card

Physics. Mass in integer units, conserved at every tick between soil, food and bodies. One sun crosses the torus; food grows in its light where the soil is rich and decays back to soil; its scent diffuses a few cells. Rock cannot be entered; mud keeps less than half of every step. A body is a graph of point masses and springs; muscles are springs whose rest length follows a motor port; each node keeps a share of its displacement along its head-ward axis, less against it and less across it, which is how a shape crawls. Bodies push each other, a mouth can bite the body ahead, and a bitten body with no energy left loses a node. The dead become food where they lie.

Inherited. The body graph with its grips, the clock's frequency, the split threshold, the drives, the size of the records, the learning rates, the planning horizon, and the slow weights of two record patches: a policy that proposes the motor from the reading, the clock and the previous motor, and a model that predicts the change of the reading and the energy change from the reading, the clock and the motor. A child is born with three nodes and grows the rest one unit of mass at a time. Every genome is mutated at birth: node positions, grips, nodes added or removed, muscles gained or lost, and small steps on the slow weights.

Learned in one life. The records of both patches: what the slow readout got wrong at each reading, written after every window of eight ticks. The slow weights move by the adjoint of the same window at the inherited rate, which may be zero. The planner repairs the motor ports of a private path under the model toward the drives, executes the first repaired motor, and the policy is taught that repair. Records are never inherited.

Price. A base rate, per node, per unit of muscle work, per unit read, per record cell, per write, per planning replay, and per slow weight while learning, each paid as the probability of losing one unit of mass to the soil. Nothing else selects.

More. Every rule with its numbers is on the rules page.

Brain. The patches are the JavaScript twin of cadence.RecordPatchNet, checked against the library to 1e-15 on observation, windows, backtracking and planning. The records here use a sparse projection with eight inputs per cell; the library's dense projection is the reference. In the inspection view every line is a slow weight and shines with the signal it carries this tick; the grid is the record cells, the lit ones are the reading's code. The founders come from the gait lab, an explicit search outside the world that the world never sees again.
