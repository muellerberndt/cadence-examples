Cadence Chorale

A patch net that learned Bach chorales chord by chord. Give it eight chords of an opening; it settles once per beat and writes the next chord from what its 54 pitch owners come to rest at. Press play to hear it.

Grey: the given opening. Green: what the net wrote.

Output owners, last chord

54 pitch owners; the ones above the threshold sound, at most four.

What this is

Each beat, the last eight chords are clamped onto 8 × 54 input owners (one per pitch per beat). The net settles from rest; every output owner above the threshold is a pitch in the next chord. No recurrence: nothing is carried but the window.

It learned from 229 chorales, transposed up and down three semitones, with the quadratic nudge on a multi-hot target: several owners can be right at once.
