Cadence examples

Brains that learn through one stream of experience: observe, remember, predict, act, learn from the outcome. Each example ships its page, its acceptance receipt and the verifier that recomputes the receipt from the event logs.

Arm

A two-link arm learns its own body from motor babbling, reaches targets through a search over the consequences it has recorded and copies what you draw, with the whole brain live beside it.

World

In a 6 by 6 world seen one cell at a time, one life learns the consequences of its actions, remembers where it saw an object, corrects that memory, holds a cue across a delay and grounds words in objects.

Artist

The arm holds a pen over a canvas, learns what its strokes leave and draws figures it has never seen, replanning from its own canvas after every stroke. Draw a figure and watch it drawn, with the whole brain live beside the canvas.

Connect Four

One life learns what a dropped stone does and which windows are completed lines from the games it plays, and searches over what it learned. Play against it while it keeps learning, with the whole brain live beside the board.
