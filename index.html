Cadence Examples

A ladder of things a patch net can learn with one owner-local rule: two settlements and one comparison per batch, no backward pass. Every rung has a receipt that binds its numbers to its code and data, measured next to a same-sized backprop model on the same split. Two rungs are games you can play; the net settles in the page for every move.
