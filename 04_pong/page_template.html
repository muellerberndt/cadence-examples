What this is

Each step the current frame and the previous one are clamped onto 384 input owners. The net settles from rest; the most active of three output owners (up, stay, down) moves its paddle one pixel. No search, no memory beyond that one earlier frame.

It was trained by playing: after each rollout, the actions that paid were nudged toward and the actions that cost were nudged away, and every overlap moved on its own two-phase difference.
