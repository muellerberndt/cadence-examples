Brain size and speech over time

Lineages

Per tick, per cell: with probability 0.08 × light a unit of soil becomes food; with 0.01 food becomes soil; with 0.05 a unit of soil steps to a neighbour. Food is drawn as height.

A creature reads a window of cells, its energy, the light and what it last did, as one binary reading. A fixed sparse expansion, developed from its genome, turns the reading into a few active cells; each active cell holds one record per action of the mass that followed. It acts on the mean of those records, and after the outcome writes the error into exactly those cells. The genome carries the window radius, the number of cells, how many fire, the learning rate, the horizon, the exploration, and the number of symbols it can utter; a split mutates it.

Each tick a creature loses a unit to the soil under it with probability 0.08 + 0.0004 per reading unit + 0.01 per 256 cells + 0.002 per active cell + 0.05 if it moved + 0.02 if it spoke. At 24 units it splits. At 0 it dies and its records leave the known world.
