Ledger

The brain on the right is the whole agent: sensory and goal neurons drive the workspace, the workspace couples to the dynamics cortex, which reads the efference copy of the action; the motor neurons hang off the workspace; the context neurons carry a trace of the last workspace state. The world model is the records cortex beside the scan (cerebellum-like, dentate-like): the reading of the sensory, goal, action and recall neurons goes through a fixed random expansion, the most active granules of 32,000 are kept (the lit cells), and each active cell's records of the next displacement, velocity change and angle change are read out; when the outcome arrives it is written into exactly those records (the flash), rate-scaled. Imagined consequences for the planner are record reads (the grey flickers). Every settling step of every phase of the settled regions (free, the free phase after learning, the bootstrap value) is drawn as it happens; synapses flash when an update moves them. Decisions come from a bounded search through the model's own imagined consequences, or, with probability ε, a random torque. Nothing here is scripted: the arm reaches only as well as the model it has learned lets it.
