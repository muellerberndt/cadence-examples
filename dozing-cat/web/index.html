The dozing cat

A cat dozes on the sill, eyes half closed, its brain running on one cheap moment per decision; a dot where it expected nothing surprises it, it wakes, chases with its paw, and dozes again when the sill is quiet.Click the sill to switch the laser on, move the mouse to lead the cat, hold still for a moment to let it catch the dot, click again to switch the laser off.
