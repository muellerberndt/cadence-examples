Cadence Examples

Small patch-net examples with explicit memory, local feedback and evidence receipts. The first four pages retain historical trained artifacts. Updating memory compares residual writes with additive memory, exact retrieval and a trained transformer; each tutorial states its assumptions and limits. Publicly hosted copies are historical.
