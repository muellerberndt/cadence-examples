Cadence Examples

A ladder of things a patch net can learn with one owner-local rule: two settlements and one comparison per batch, no backward pass. Every rung has a receipt that binds its numbers to its code and data, measured next to the strongest backprop model in the same script, and stays only while the receipt shows the patch net ahead of it on a primary measure. Two rungs are pages you can play: Pong, and a chorale writer with sound; the net settles in the page for every move.
