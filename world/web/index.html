What this brain can do

  • Remembers where it saw each object and walks there from memory under a deadline, from a cell at least four steps away.
  • Corrects a stale memory when it sees the object moved or the place empty. Drag an object to another cell to test it.
  • Holds a cue across a delay of 8 to 32 steps and acts on it at the junction.
  • Grounds words in objects. Teach it a word and the word becomes a request.
  • Keeps navigating when the doors stop opening, relearning the passages from what it sees.
  • One stream, no replay ring. The whole brain runs in your browser.

What plays here

A 6 by 6 world of walls, doors, four objects, four colours and eight words, seen one cell at a time, and one Cadence brain. Sensory and goal neurons drive a workspace; recall neurons carry what the stores read (the remembered place, the cue held, a word's referent); the workspace couples to a dynamics cortex; motor neurons hang off the workspace; context neurons carry a trace of the last state. The world model is a records cortex whose cells hold the next displacement, passages, cell contents, carried object, outcome and reward. Four declared stores keep witnessed evidence: object to place, cell to passages, the last word seen, word to referent.

Measured

Acceptance receipt, five seeds. Requests fulfilled 100% with memory, 18% with the place records erased · visible correction 100% · cue at delay 8 100% (reward-shifted control 51%) · grounding 100% · door recovery 100% · shuffled action pairing 3% · random moves 1%. Same life, world model swapped: cue at delay 8 52% for an online transformer and 59% for an online MLP, with or without a replay ring; consequence accuracy 93% to 95% against 98% for the records brain.

How it works

Every action comes from the brain's step. On a request the planner searches over cells through the consequences the records imagine for each action, toward the remembered place; a known cell takes its passages from the witnessed map, an unvisited one from the model's own prediction. During a wander the actions are random and the sightings are the brain's own. The outcome of every decision is written into exactly the records that read it, so the model keeps learning while you watch.
