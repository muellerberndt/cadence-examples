Ledger

The brain on the right is the whole agent: sensory and goal neurons drive the workspace, the recall neurons carry what the stores read (the remembered place of the requested object, the cue held, the word's referent strengths), the workspace couples to the dynamics cortex, which reads the efference copy of the action; the motor neurons hang off the workspace; the context neurons carry a trace of the last workspace state. The world model is the records cortex beside the scan (cerebellum-like, dentate-like): the reading of the sensory, goal, action and recall neurons minus each unit's running mean goes through a fixed random expansion, the most active granules of 16,000 are kept (the lit cells), and each active cell's records of the next displacement, passages, cell contents, carried object, outcome and reward are read out; when the outcome arrives it is written into exactly those records (the flash; reward writes in pink). Imagined consequences for the search are record reads (the grey flickers); a known next cell takes its passages from the witnessed map. Every settling step of every phase of the settled regions (free, the free phase after learning, the bootstrap value) is drawn as it happens; synapses flash when an update moves them. Decisions come from a best-first search through the model's imagined consequences toward a remembered or exploration target, from one-step predicted reward at the cue junction, or, during a wander, a random legal action. Nothing here is scripted: the agent reaches only what its stores and its learned model let it reach.
