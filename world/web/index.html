The world. Six by six cells with walls, doors, four objects, four colours and eight words, permuted for each life. The agent reads its own cell, the four passages around it, what it carries and the outcome of its last action; the four neighbouring cells enter the reading only in the moment after an inspect. Asking for an object starts a new episode of the same life from a cell at least four steps away, with a deadline of twice the shortest path plus two. The stores cross that boundary and the context trace is cleared, so a request is reached in time only from a remembered place. Dragging an object to another cell changes the world under the agent: the place it wrote down stays until it stands there and sees the cell empty, which erases the record, or sees the object somewhere else, which overwrites it.

The decisions. Every action comes from the agent's step. On a request the planner searches over cells through the consequences the records cortex imagines for each action, at most 128 expansions deep, toward the remembered place; a known cell takes its passages from the witnessed map, an unvisited one from the model's own prediction. At the cue junction the choice is one-step reward greed over the model's reward prediction. During a wander the exploration rate is one, so the actions are random and the sightings are the agent's own. The outcome of every decision is written into the records of the cells that read it, so the model keeps learning while you watch.

The brain. Sensory and goal neurons drive the workspace; the recall neurons carry what the stores read (the remembered place of the requested object, the cue held, the word's referent strengths); the workspace couples to the dynamics cortex, which reads the efference copy of the action; the motor neurons hang off the workspace; the context neurons carry a trace of the last workspace state. The world model is the records cortex beside the scan: the reading of the sensory, goal, action and recall neurons minus each unit's running mean goes through a fixed random expansion, the most active cells are kept (the lit ones), and their records of the next displacement, passages, cell contents, carried object, outcome and reward are read out; the outcome is written into exactly those cells (the flash, pink for reward). The imagined reads of the search flicker grey. Every settling step of every phase is drawn as it happens.
