What this brain can do

  • Learned what its pen leaves on the canvas by scribbling. Nothing about drawing was programmed.
  • Draws figures it has never seen, one stroke at a time, from a target you draw.
  • Looks at its own canvas after every stroke and replans from what the pen actually left.
  • Keeps drawing when its body and its canvas change mid-drawing: a longer link, an offset canvas.
  • Two levels of search read one learned model: a stroke intention, then the torques that follow it.
  • One stream, no replay ring. A decision takes 75 ms at the p95, inside the 100 ms it has.

What plays here

The arm of the first example holds a pen over a 32 by 32 canvas, and one Cadence brain. Sensory and goal neurons drive a workspace; the workspace couples to a dynamics cortex that reads the efference copy of the action; motor neurons hang off the workspace. The world model is a records cortex: the body senses, the pen state, the ink of the last decision, the canvas and target windows around the pen, the intention and the candidate action pass through a fixed random expansion, and each active cell holds records of the next displacement, velocity, angle and ink window. The grey flickers on the raster are the search reading records it never writes.

Measured

Acceptance receipt, five seeds. Held-out foreground F1 0.93 at a Chamfer distance of 0.014 canvas diagonals · weakest figure family 0.91 · after a longer link 0.93, back on the original body 0.93. Controls: the same architecture frozen from birth 0.00 · random scribbling 0.08 · the supplied path oracle 0.64 · an online MLP with a replay ring behind the same search 0.80. Decision latency p95 75 ms.

How it works

Your strokes are rasterised one pixel wide as the target. Every tenth of a second the artist commits one of eighteen choices, nine torque pairs by pen up or down. The slow level of the search asks the records what one decision of motion leaves for each direction and pen state, repeats that along a candidate stroke and keeps the best over a beam of four; the fast level imagines all eighteen choices two decisions deep and executes the best. The intention is renewed every second decision from the canvas the artist sees. A drawing ends under a discrepancy of 0.010, at 200 decisions, or after 60 decisions without improvement.
