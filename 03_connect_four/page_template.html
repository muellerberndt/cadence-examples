What this is

The board reaches the net as 84 clamped input owners: its own discs and yours. The net settles under that clamp. With lookahead enabled, the game rules evaluate future moves and the learned outputs break ties. The win records below include that search; they are not the strength of the network alone.

It first imitates a teacher, then plays and revisits teacher examples on positions where its choices disagree. Its weights change through free/nudged local updates. Local learning mode retains lessons between sessions.
