What this is

The board reaches the net as 84 clamped input owners: its own discs and yours. The net settles under that clamp with no search and no lookahead; the most active output owner among the legal columns is its move.

It learned by imitating a depth-4 search on self-play positions, with the free/nudged rule: two settlements and one local comparison per batch, no backward pass.
