What this is

Each pixel's grey level, 0 to 16, is the clamp on its input owner. From rest, every owner moves its potential toward its total drive until no activation changes by more than 10⁻⁴; the bars replay the ten output owners step by step.

The net learned with the free/nudged rule: for every batch, a settlement under the picture, a settlement nudged toward the label and one nudged away, and every seam moving on the difference of its own two endpoint products. No backward pass. The hand-drawn digits are off the training distribution (scikit-learn's were scanned and blurred), so expect misreadings on a thin stroke.
