WHAT THIS PAGE IS. A recorded life, replayed. The three files under every piece are what the emulated SID played: the demonstration is the piece as the environment plays it, the imitation is what the composer played back at it after hearing it, and the continuation is what it played on, having heard the first half only. The brain beside them replays the settled activation of all its neurons, block by block, from the episode that made that sound, and the records cortex replays the code, the imagined reads and the writes of the same blocks. While the demonstration plays, the brain is listening: those frames are the last listening pass of the same episode, the whole phrase once. Nothing on this page is computed from a brain running in the browser: the chip is a cycle-exact emulator with no port in a browser yet, and a composer without its instrument makes no sound. When one runs there, the composer plays live on this page, as the arm, the world, the artist and Connect Four already do.

THE LANES. Each lane is the level of the file the page is playing, one bar per 20 ms block over -60 dBFS to 0, filling as the audio reaches it. It is measured off that file and nothing else. The notes each voice played are measured by the stage and are absent from this bundle; a bundle that carries them turns these lanes into a three-voice roll.

THE INSTRUMENT. One gesture is 28 integers over 541 motor neurons: per voice a pitch, a pulse width, a waveform, a gate and four envelope rates, and shared between them a cutoff, a resonance, a routing and a volume. A block is 20 ms, a sixteenth is 6 blocks, a bar is 96 blocks and a phrase is 2 bars, which is 125 beats a minute. The chip is obeyed: a repeated note needs a block with the gate down, and a sustain raised while the gate is up silences that voice until the gate falls again. The body supplies no melody, no bass pattern, no voice role and no chord progression.

WHAT IT HEARS. Four channels of 68 numbers every block: 64 log bands from 100 Hz to 10 kHz, the level, the onset and a silence flag, measured on the mix and on each voice's own monitor. The demonstration is heard and never read. In the listen phase the phrase is played four times, one voice at a time in the corpus's voice order and then the whole phrase, so the reader hears one note where a mix carries three. That change took pitch within a semitone from 0.371 to 0.508 on the development seeds and moved nothing else.

THE BRAIN. Sensory neurons carry the hearing, the workspace couples to the dynamics cortex that reads the efference copy of the gesture, and the motor neurons hang off the workspace. The world model is the records cortex beside the scan: the hearing and the executed gesture go through a fixed random expansion, the most active granules are kept (the lit cells), and each active cell's records of the next change of every hearing field are read out. When the block's outcome arrives it is written into exactly those cells (the flash). The grey flickers are the planner reading records it never writes.

THE MATERIAL. The seven pieces here are short pieces written for this stage by tools/make_sample.py and placed in the public domain by it: a two-bar ostinato, a variant of it in the same family, a piece whose voices rest at different times, one voice alone, a fast arpeggio, an A-B-A form and a bass line. Their numbers are the numbers of seven short pieces. The run's numbers are in the gate table, over the held-out split of the private corpus: a piece of two bars holds few enough notes that one note moves a number of it a great deal. The corpus the life learned from and was measured on is private and stays private: the HVSC register performances carry the collection's own copyright notice, and the renderings of the Lakh clean MIDI arrangements carry the compositions' rights whatever the dataset's licence says. Neither is redistributed, here or anywhere. The sound is the MOS 8580 as pyresidfp 0.17.0 emulates it, at 22,050 Hz, mono, sixteen bit, from the build the receipt pins file by file.
