Waiting for a captured settling

Every neuron and every synapse is drawn as one brain scan: regions sit where their connections place them, tissue brightness follows the chosen signal, the glow marks neurons that changed between recorded iterations, and messages travel along the synapses of active neurons. Wheel or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Live traces sample the actual settling of candidates. Traces replay sampled simulation steps. Brightness and messages are scaled to the largest activation, repair and mismatch of this captured trace. These are model signals, not EEG or neurotransmitter concentrations.

Inside this brain

The auditory history, harmony, rhythm, phrase context and note intention regions settle together. Changed messages alter neighboring synaptic inputs; those neurons repair their state in turn. A finite settling budget does not guarantee a unique or globally best equilibrium.

What it learned—and what we supplied

Reading checkpoint receipt…

Learned: event predictions and abstract harmony, interval and groove relationships from MIDI music. Supplied: keyword mood/style controls, tonal and four-bar structure, motif readback, voice-leading preferences, accompaniment and orchestration. “Cinematic” is a broad musical setting, not an artist imitation model.

The composer tests multiple continuations, scores them, synthesizes the draft and inspects its waveform. Revisions must improve the explicit score and pass audio health checks. Human-level musical judgment and a transformer efficiency advantage have not been established.
