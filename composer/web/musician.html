Every neuron and every synapse is drawn as one brain scan: regions sit where their connections place them, the glow marks neurons that changed between iterations, and messages travel along the synapses of active neurons. Frames are exact replays of the settling that decided each event: activation, its change per iteration (the repair) and every neuron's equation mismatch. The prefrontal region is the working-memory trace of the phrase cortex from the events before; the recall region is the form record of this bar in the previous sixteen-bar cycle. The valence is the chosen future's advantage over its rivals; the surprise is the brain's own prediction error on each event. Model quantities, not EEG.
